Orders are processed by our Merchant of Record, [[PADDLE / LEMON SQUEEZY]]. Refunds are issued by them
to the original payment method. To request a refund, write to [[EMAIL]] or use the link in your receipt.

## Coin packs
- **Unused coins:** you can request a full refund within **14 days** of purchase if none of the purchased
  coins have been spent.
- **Used coins:** coins are digital content delivered immediately. At checkout you agree to immediate
  delivery and acknowledge that you lose the statutory right of withdrawal once you start using the coins.
  We may still refund the unused part at our discretion.
- Coins earned for free (lessons, streaks, bonuses) are never refundable.

## Subscriptions (Plus, Family)
- You can cancel at any time in Settings or through the link in your receipt.
  You keep access until the end of the current billing period, and no further charges are made.
- **First purchase:** if you are not happy, you can ask for a full refund within **14 days** of your first
  subscription payment.
- Renewals are generally not refunded, but contact us if you were charged by mistake
  (for example you forgot to cancel and did not use the Service after the renewal). We review such cases fairly.
- If a refund is granted, subscription benefits end, and bonus coins from that period may be removed.

## Technical problems
If a paid feature did not work and we could not fix it, we will return the coins spent or refund the payment.

## Chargebacks
Please contact us before opening a chargeback — we can usually solve the problem faster.
Accounts with fraudulent chargebacks may be suspended.

## Statutory rights
This policy does not limit your rights under applicable consumer law.
