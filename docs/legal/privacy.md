## 1. Data we process
| Data | Why | Legal basis |

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

## 2. Children
Children under 16 (or the local age of digital consent) may use EngLear only with the consent of a parent or guardian.
We collect only what is needed for learning. Children cannot make purchases. A teacher sees the progress
only of students linked to them. Parents can ask us to access or delete their child's data at any time.

## 4. Retention
- Account and learning data: while your account exists; deleted within 30 days after account deletion.
- Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires (usually up to 10 years).
- Technical logs: up to 90 days.

## 6. Cookies and local storage
We use only what the Service needs to work (keeping you signed in, remembering your settings).
We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If this changes, we will ask for your consent first.

## 7. Security
Access to data is restricted by database-level rules, answers are graded on the server,
and all connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

## 8. Changes
